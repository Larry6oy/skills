# Campaign Log: The Kingdom Above

## Session 1: The Skyward Gaze
- **Party:** Kriv (Lvl 6), Elara (Lvl 6), Felix (Lvl 6), Aegis-7 (Lvl 5).
- **Location:** Aethelgard.
- **Time:** Day 33 (Morning).
- **Context:** The Wardens have secured the surface borders and are now looking to the sky for potential threats or allies.

### Objectives
1.  **Intel:** Gather information on the "Kingdom Above" (Location, inhabitants, technology).
2.  **Preparation:** Retrofit *The Stormbreaker* for high-altitude/planar travel.
3.  **Side Quest:** Resolve Nezznar's status (The Black Spider).

### Log
- **Tasking:** Kriv assigned research duties.
    - **Felix:** Royal Library (Arcane/History).
    - **Elara:** Bardic College (Myths/Legends).
    - **Kriv:** Handling Nezznar (The Black Spider).
- **Encounter:** Kriv visited **Nezznar** in the Royal Library.
- **Dialogue:** Nezznar acknowledged the defeat of the Cult.
- **Status:** Nezznar is requesting the fulfillment of the "Future Employment" offer.
- **Offer:** Kriv presented 3 options: Freedom, Military, or Court (Advisor/Noble).
- **Choice:** Nezznar chose **The Court** ("A viper pit suits me").
- **Appointment:** Kriv granted Nezznar a minor noble title and advisory position.
- **Intel:** Nezznar shared lore on the Kingdom Above:
    - **Cloudstone:** The material used to anchor floating cities.
    - **Inhabitants:** Cloud Giants and Djinn.
    - **Requirement:** Proficiency in **Auran** (Language of Air) recommended.
- **Action:** Kriv handed Nezznar the keys to his manacles.
- **Event:** Nezznar released. He has assumed the persona of **Lord Nezznar**.
- **Status:** Nezznar is now an ally/asset in the Royal Court.
- **Status:** Research/Recruitment Phase Complete.
- **Travel:** Party regrouped at **The Hearth & Anvil**.
- **Debriefing:** Felix and Elara reported findings.
- **Intel (Felix):** **The Zephyr Gate** (Portal to the Kingdom) is located above the **Spine of the World** (North).
- **Intel (Elara):** **The Tithe of the Storm**. Gatekeepers require proof of mastery over air/lightning (Storm Cores).
- **Hazard:** High Altitude "Breathless Ascent" (Thin air/Cold).
- **Plan:** Fly *The Stormbreaker* to the Zephyr Gate.
- **Status:** Mission Briefing.
- **Planning:** Party brainstormed gifts for "Sky People" (Cloud Giants/Djinn).
    - **Ideas:** Fine Wine/Food (Cloud Giants love excess), Solid/Heavy items (Novelty in air), Thunder Lances (Tech trade), Furs/Warmth.
    - **Action:** Shopping trip organized.
- **Status:** Shopping / Preparation.
